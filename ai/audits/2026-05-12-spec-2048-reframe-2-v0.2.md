| D-01  | BLOCKER   | §5.3 / §5.1      | splitmix64 constants (`0x9E3779B97F4A7C15`, `0xBF58476D1CE4E5B9`, `0x94D049BB133111EB`) all exceed `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` (2^53 − 1). The natural reading is `:game/new` puts the FSM into `:playing` directly (skipping `:fresh`) with the new board seeded. | Pick one and state it: either (a) `:game/new` from any state goes straight to `:playing` (with the handler doing the spawn-twice step before the transition fires), or (b) `:game/new` goes to `:fresh` and a follow-up event/effect (`:game/initialise`?) transitions `:fresh → :playing`. ## Auditor's overall read

v0.2 is materially stronger than v0.1. All three v0.1 BLOCKERS are properly resolved: §5.2 is uniformly Malli; the wire surface is now schematised end-to-end (30+ named schemas, comfortably covering A-02's "~17 shapes" gap); and §8.2's palette is inline with full hex codes. Of the 14 v0.1 DEFECTS, 13 are fully resolved and 1 (B-07, FSM completeness) is resolved with residual issues that I've re-raised as D-04/D-05. Of the 11 NITS, 10 are absorbed; C-10 (pinning re-frame2 references to a SHA) was skipped and is re-raised as N-01.

The new BLOCKER (D-01) is the splitmix64 platform-precision problem. The framework choice (re-frame2 + Reagent + shadow-cljs) pins this spec to a CLJS target, and CLJS numbers are IEEE-754 doubles. The §5.3 constants exceed `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` by ~12 bits, so the literal Clojure snippet will silently lose precision under `unchecked-multiply`, breaking the §9.4 "Determinism" property test outright and biasing the §3.4 spawn probabilities. The fix is mechanical (switch to `goog.math.Long` or pick a 32-bit PRNG) but it is load-bearing and must be made before this spec dispatches to an implementing agent.

The remaining DEFECTS cluster in three areas: (i) the FSM is "exhaustive" on transitions but not on ignored events (D-04) and contains one synthetic double-transition (D-05); (ii) the `:storage/save` and `Fx-StorageWrite` shapes are slightly under-specified after the §5.2 rewrite (D-06, D-07); (iii) the spawn / lose-detection / `:game/move` boundary is unclear about events-vs-sub-functions (D-08). None of these block an implementing agent the way A-01..A-03 in v0.1 did — they will just produce 1-3 small follow-up beads as the implementer encounters them.

The Malli rewrite is clean. I spot-checked roughly 20 schemas for parse-correctness and found one substantive cross-spec contradiction (`Cell` hard-coding 4×4 — D-02) and one wrong-tool-named (`malli.instrument` for db validation — D-09). The new §5.3 algorithmically matches Vigna, with only the value-choice prose needing a rewrite (D-03).

Recommendation: fix D-01 inline (this one really does block), fix D-02..D-05 in the same pass (cheap), leave D-06..D-09 for the operator if time-boxed, nits can ride. After D-01..D-05 are addressed, v0.3 is ready for dispatch.
